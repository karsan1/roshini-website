## Context

Rocks can retain magnetic signals from the time they formed. Those records provide one route to reconstructing the geodynamo during intervals of major environmental and biological change. The Ediacaran Period is especially significant because of its position in Earth's climatic and biological history.

## What I do

I analyze paleointensity and paleomagnetic data from metarhyolites of the Catoctin Volcanic Province. The work evaluates whether the preserved remanence can reliably constrain field behavior during the Ediacaran.

Laboratory measurements include thermal demagnetization and paleointensity experiments. I also evaluate magnetite and hematite as potential magnetic carriers using low-temperature MPMS measurements, First Order Reversal Curves, and petrographic observations.

Geospatial work in QGIS connects samples with available outcrop and geochronologic context.

## Current scope

This work is ongoing. The site does not present preliminary measurements as finalized findings.
