## Context

Wet-lab work provides practical grounding for thinking about how organisms interact with environmental and mineral conditions relevant to habitability studies.

## What I did

During this research-shadowing experience, I prepared custom marine media and worked with *Shewanella* cultures. The workflow included inoculation, culture preparation, monitoring growth, and maintaining laboratory documentation.

## Scope

This experience is presented as supervised research exposure. It is not described as an independent research project or as evidence of a specific biological finding.
