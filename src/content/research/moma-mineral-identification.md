## Context

The Mars Organic Molecule Analyser (MOMA) is designed to investigate molecular and mineralogical information on Mars. Mineral context can strengthen the interpretation of measurements without being treated as autonomous biosignature detection.

## What I did

I worked with laser-desorption mass spectrometry datasets containing more than 4,000 mass spectra per dataset and approximately 500 mass-to-charge ratios. I built Python workflows to organize the data and train Random Forest models across 13 mineral classes.

I evaluated model behavior using accuracy measurements and confusion matrices, with attention to which mineral classes were readily separated and which were more frequently confused.
